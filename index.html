Built on Plus Jakarta Sans, Bhinneka Sans maps Indonesia’s five major island regions to five vowels based on their frequency in Bahasa Indonesia and regional population size. Each of the 38 letterforms represents a province’s cultural heritage, inviting visitors to explore and combine them into their own compositions.
